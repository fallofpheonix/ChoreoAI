# Submission Guidelines & Constraints: ChoreoAI

## What to Build (Deliverables)
1. **Dynamic Point-Cloud Dataset**: Extract motion capture poses from dance videos into dynamic 3D point-cloud data.
2. **Semi-supervised Modality Paradigm**: Construct paired modalities corresponding to the dance movements (natural language descriptions, spoken word, music, visual art, 3D architectural renderings, and more).
3. **Multimodal Embedding Scheme**: Design the embedding space mapping these diverse dimensions.
4. **Any-to-any Generative Model**: Train a model able to translate input from one arbitrary modality (e.g., text) into 3D movement, and vice-versa.

## What to Submit to Mentor
**CRITICAL**: The submission rules for ChoreoAI are DIFFERENT from standard HumanAI projects.
- Please email your submission to **human.ai.choreo@gmail.com** (NOT cern.ch).
- Subject line MUST be: **"Test Submission: AI Choreo"**
- Include your CV and a link to your GitHub repository.
- **Do NOT contact mentors directly.**

## Constraints
- **Required Stack**: Python, Git, Numpy, Matplotlib, Pandas. Previous ML experience required (TensorFlow or PyTorch).
- **Artistic Interest**: Strong personal interest in performing arts and open-ended aesthetic dimensions.
- **Difficulty**: Hard (175 hours).
